Deep Field Front door · 30 Aug 2026

There is no home screen — the game opens on a file picker

Deep Field boots straight into Choose a sector, spends its largest type on an instruction, hides its entire second game mode in a 12px ghost link, and describes three boards in prose while the actual map data sits one function call away. This is the fix.

On the running build. 100.96.85.82:8787 is inside your tailnet and not reachable from here, so this was read from src/ui/menuScreen.ts, lobbyScreen.ts, app/progress.ts and app/raceSeries.ts at main. Every screen, string and stored field described below is the live one. If the running build has drifted from main, tell me what looks different.

1 · What the front door is now

The flow

boot → Choose a sector → difficulty + seed → Launch Race a friend → (ghost link, top-right)

What is already good and must survive

  • Unlock state is derived, never stored. A level is open because the previous one was cleared. No second source of truth, no player locked out of a level they finished.
  • Records are per-difficulty and honest — grade, lives kept out of the reserve they came from, simulated seconds, waves. Defeats are recorded too but unlock nothing.
  • Road length is parsed, not written down. parseMap walks the tiles and the result is cached. A board cannot lie about its own shape.
  • Storage is defensive throughout. Private-mode Safari, a full quota, a future version's JSON — all degrade to "nothing cleared yet" and none of them stop the game booting.
  • Difficulty is remembered across sessions, so the usual choice is one click.

2 · Four problems

Problem 1
The biggest type in the game is an instruction

mn-title is 56px and it reads "Choose a sector". The game never says its own name at size, never says what it is, and gives a first-time player a grid with one card open and two locked.

Problem 2
Half the game is a ghost link

Race — the mode with a relay server, a lobby, a countdown, a wire protocol and a running head-to-head record — is 12px, #75798c, top-right, in the weakest button style the stylesheet has.

Problem 3
The boards are described, not shown

"A symmetric double hairpin around a pocket that reaches both lanes" is good writing about a shape the player could simply be shown. The map rows are already imported and parseMap is already being called on them.

Problem 4
Returning is as slow as arriving

The most common intent — carry on from where I was, at the difficulty I always play — takes two screens and a read. furthestUnlocked and lastDifficulty are both already computed; nothing acts on them.

And one thing collected but never shown. raceSeries.ts keeps a win–loss–tie record per opponent name in localStorage and formats it as "Series vs Vela: 3–2". It appears nowhere on the front door. A standing record against a named friend is the single strongest reason to open this game twice, and it is invisible until you are already mid-lobby.

Problem

Boot lands on a picker. The most common intent costs two screens; the game never names itself; the second mode is a link; and the strongest retention hook in the codebase — a running record against a named friend — is invisible here.

Change

A new homeScreen.ts ahead of menuScreen. Continue reads furthestUnlocked + lastDifficulty; the Race card reads raceSeries; the backdrop is the resumed sector's own map. No new stored state.

Must hold

The board behind the title must not out-glow the type — it is at 0.5 with a directional scrim over it. And Continue must never be wrong: if progress is empty or unreadable it is absent, not a dead button (see 3.1).

Problem

Must hold

Locked sectors still exist and must still be discoverable — they live on the campaign screen, where a locked card is information rather than the first impression.

Problem

Each card spends a 62px-minimum block of prose describing a shape, then makes the player go to a second screen to pick a difficulty they almost always pick the same way. Locked cards hide the board entirely, so there is nothing to want.

Change

A boardThumb(map) helper emitting SVG rects from row-runs — the same merge the mocks here were generated with, ~20 rects a board. Difficulty segmented onto the card, seeded from lastDifficulty. Blurb moves to a hover tooltip / expanded state.

Must hold

Road length stays parsed — never a hand-written number. Turn count derives from waypoints.length - 2, same discipline. And the thumbnail must use the route's real brightness ramp, so the front door teaches the board's visual grammar before the player ever sees it at size.

Problem

An advanced field is given permanent primary space and thirty-one words of justification, in the path of every launch.

Change

Collapse behind a button. Store the last-used seed alongside lastDifficulty and surface it as a one-click replay — which is the case the paragraph was describing.

Must hold

Blank still means random, and a typed seed still reproduces exactly. The field is hidden, never removed — it is how a shared board gets handed between two people.

7 · Acceptance checklist

✓SurfaceTest
☐Time to resumeA returning player with one sector cleared is in a running game in one click from boot.
☐Continue honestyWith localStorage cleared, disabled, or holding a future version's JSON, the home screen renders the first-run variant — no dead Continue, no crash.
☐Mode parityCampaign and Race are the same visual weight. A player who has never raced can find it without reading the top bar.
☐Board recognitionA player shown the three thumbnails, then dropped onto one of the boards, can say which sector they are on.
☐No prose requiredEvery sector card is understandable with its blurb hidden: shape, length, waves, turns, difficulty, best grade.
☐First runA first-time player sees no locked card and no zero-score before their first game. One primary action, named.
☐Series visibilityA player with a race history sees the record on the home screen without navigating.
☐Relay honestyWith the server down, the Race card says so before the player types a name — and the status dot never claims "up" on a stale probe.
☐KeyboardHome → campaign → difficulty → launch is completable without a mouse, with a visible focus ring at every step.
☐Parsed factsRoad length and turn count still derive from the map. Changing a map's rows changes the card with no other edit.
☐Destructive distanceReset progress is not reachable in one click from any screen containing a Launch button.

Sequence

  1. Board thumbnails (boardThumb) — smallest change, biggest visible gain, and every other item uses it.
  2. Home screen, both variants, with Continue and the two mode cards.
  3. Sector cards rebuilt on the thumbnail, difficulty inline, seed collapsed.
  4. Race card wired to raceSeries and a relay probe.
  5. Keyboard, Settings, How to play — the tail, once the shape is settled.

Open

  • Should Continue resume the furthest unlocked sector, or the last one actually played? They differ after a player goes back to farm a better grade. Last-played is more literal; furthest is more often what is wanted.
  • Does the relay probe belong on the home screen at all, or does it make an offline single-player boot feel broken? A dot that says "relay down" on a machine with no server running may be worse than no dot.
